SoundTrip privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Applies to the SoundTrip Android app and website.

What stays on your device

SoundTrip is a player for music files you import yourself. Audio you import, its title, artist, album, artwork and lyrics, your playlists, moods, favourites, playback queue and settings are stored only on your device. On the website they are stored in your browser's storage for this site. SoundTrip does not upload your audio files anywhere.

Spotify playlist exports you choose to import are read on your device. SoundTrip keeps only playlist names and song titles, artists, albums and Spotify links. Other account details in the export are ignored and never stored or sent anywhere.

What SoundTrip collects

The published Android app collects no personal data. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not share data with third parties. It asks for no storage, microphone, location or contacts permission: files are chosen by you through Android's system file picker.

Optional online features

Some builds can offer optional account sync or an official Spotify playlist connection. These are switched off in the published Android app. If a future version turns them on, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is sent, where, and how to delete it.

"Open in Spotify" buttons open Spotify's own app or website, where Spotify's privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Delete playlists or imported Spotify exports inside the app. To delete everything SoundTrip stored, including imported audio, clear its storage in Android's app settings or uninstall it. Android app backup is disabled, so SoundTrip data is not copied to your Google account.

Children

SoundTrip is not directed at children under 13.
